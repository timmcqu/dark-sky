Remote ID, ADS-B, and how we sell.

Short answers. Receive-only. If people are in danger, call 911 first.

Does a Remote ID receiver see every drone?

No. Cooperative broadcasts only. Dark or RID-off aircraft do not appear.

Hardware Only or Ready-to-Run?

Hardware Only is the factory kit; you load the receiver image. Ready-to-Run is the same hardware — we load it before it ships. Extra business day. Full comparison.

Raspberry Pi or ESP32?

ESP32 for pocket Remote ID. Raspberry Pi when you want ADS-B on the same kit. Pi vs ESP32.

Do you jam?

No. We do not jam, spoof, or intercept. Not a licensed security company.

Is Fusion Sensor 24/7?

No. Laptop software or a named-date coverage. Not an unattended operations center.

Can you fly this week?

Flown jobs are request and need a Part 107 PIC. Ground detection with radios is a named-date coverage and does not require a flight that day.

How fast do files arrive?

Digital SKUs open after Stripe. Hardware ships after payment from US shops, or from us after we load Ready-to-Run.
