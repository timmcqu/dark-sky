FAQ

Does the outdoor 1090 screw onto the radio?

No. The outdoor antenna is N-female. The listen radio is SMA female. The kit includes an SMA-male to N-male jumper. Do not force the outdoor stick onto the radio.

Is the box NEMA 4X?

No. It is an IP66 project box, about 5 × 7 × 3 in, with a clear lid. Honest weather rating. Not a UL-listed NEMA 4X electrical enclosure. No cable glands — you drill. The USB listen radio may need a short USB extension and a hole.

Is the power supply outdoor-rated?

No. Official 27 W USB-C is an indoor brick. Sit it inside or under cover. No battery and no UPS in this kit.

When do weekend orders ship?

Monday. Shops that fulfill this sit are closed Saturday night. We order the next business day to the address on the Stripe receipt.

How is this different from the $399 Pi 5 receiver?

That kit is an indoor official case and a 5 dBi SMA whip. This kit is a weather box, a 9 dBi N-female outdoor base, and the mating jumper. Different antenna class. No official indoor case.

Is there a 978 whip or a filter?

No. Outdoor 1090 only. For 978 and the dual-channel filter, see the $549 professional sit — that one is indoor.
