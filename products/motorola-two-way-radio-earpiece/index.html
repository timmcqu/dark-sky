FAQ

Is this a jammer or a C-UAS transmitter?

No. This is a two-way radio for field comms — push-to-talk voice. It does not jam, spoof, or send a C-UAS command. Dark Sky Systems does not sell jammers.

Do you include a Part 90 license or program frequencies?

No. The buyer (or their agency) gets and holds their own FCC license and programs their own frequencies. The radio ships at factory default. We do not sell programming and we are not a licensed radio service.

Analog and digital?

Yes. One Motorola UHF unit does analog and DMR digital. 64 channels, 4 W high / 1 W low, 400–480 MHz.

What ships in the kit?

The radio, its OEM Li-ion battery, UHF antenna, stand-up charger, wall supply, belt clip, manual, and the Motorola 2-wire acoustic-tube earpiece. The speaker microphone is a separate SKU.

Does this include the speaker microphone?

No. The speaker microphone is a separate $179 accessory that fits this radio. Add it if you want vest-clip PTT as well as the earpiece.
