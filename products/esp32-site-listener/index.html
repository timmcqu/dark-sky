FAQ

Hardware Only or Ready-to-Run?

Hardware Only is the kit as the shop ships it — you load the listener image. Ready-to-Run is the same hardware. We load the listener firmware on the board before it ships. Extra business day. Ships from us after we load it.

Does PoE give the board Ethernet?

No. The PoE brick splits 802.3af into USB-C 5 V / 2 A and a 10/100 Ethernet jack. The ESP32 board has no Ethernet. PoE here means one-cable 5 V power. You already have an 802.3af switch or injector — we do not include one. USB-C-only install is also valid.

What connector is on the board?

w.FL (u.FL / IPEX compatible), not SMA. The duck is RP-SMA. The pigtail is SMA. The adapter in the kit is required: board w.FL → pigtail → adapter → duck. Do not mate the SMA pigtail to the RP-SMA duck without the adapter.

Do you assemble the box or drill the holes?

No. The large box has no pre-cut glands. You or your installer drill for the RJ-45 and PG-9 glands. We do not assemble.

Is a battery included?

No. USB-C or PoE-to-USB-C 5 V. We refuse LiPo add-ons.

Is this a spectrum scanner?

No. An ESP32 is not a spectrum analyzer. The spectrum scanner is the TinySA Ultra on the scan page.
