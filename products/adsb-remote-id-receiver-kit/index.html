No. The $399 kit is the one with the Pi 5, official power, and official case. This kit is board, radio, two whips, and a case.

Is there a SAW filter?

No. The $279 advanced radio kit is the one with the 1090 / 978 SAW filter. This kit does not include it.

Is the radio V4?

No. V3-class USB radio with dipole. V4 is not listed.

Does it jam or transmit?

No. Receive only.

Is there a battery?
