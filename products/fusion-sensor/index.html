Dark Sky Console is the operations map on a laptop you already own. It plots aircraft that are transmitting cooperative Remote ID — RID-compliant broadcasts when the radio is on. When a listen radio is connected it also plots 1090 and 978 ADS-B. You load a friendly-list pack: serials and callsigns you own (ENG, media, venue ops). Green is on that list. Red is a RID-compliant broadcast that is not on the list. Amber is RID with no usable ID. You own the list; we do not invent friendlies. Optional fingerprint and vendor database packs, when loaded, corroborate a cooperative broadcast (make, family, prior look). They do not find a dark or RID-off aircraft. RF energy on the bands a listen radio hears can sit on the same picture as corroboration — energy is not an ID. Quiet is not a clearance. You supply the radios. We set the console up with you. Does not jam, spoof, or intercept. Not unattended 24/7.

If people are in danger, call 911 first. Receive-only. Cooperative Remote ID and ADS-B only.
