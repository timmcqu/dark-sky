FAQ

Which kit has Remote ID?

Starter, receiver, professional, and outdoor. The feeder does not — it is 1090 and 978 only. The $199 ADS-B kit has Remote ID and no computer.

Hardware Only or Ready-to-Run?

Hardware Only ships as the factory kit. You load the receiver image. Ready-to-Run is the same hardware — we load the image on a 64 GB card before it ships. Extra business day.

Is this Fusion Sensor?

No. These are Raspberry Pi receiver kits. Fusion Sensor is console software on its own page.

What radio is in the kit?

A current USB radio with dipole. SMA on the radio.
