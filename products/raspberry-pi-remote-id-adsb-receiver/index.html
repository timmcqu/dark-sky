FAQ

Is this Fusion Sensor?

No. Fusion Sensor is the $2,000 / $4,500 flashed kit we set up with you. This kit is the computer, the receive board, the radio, and the 1090 whip. You load your own software.

Is there a battery?

No. Official USB-C 27 W supply. No Li-ion and no UPS HAT.

Is the radio V4?

No. The radio in this kit is the current V3-class USB radio with dipole. V4 is not listed.

How does this differ from the $199 ADS-B kit?

The $199 kit is a board, a radio, two whips, and a small hard case — no computer. This kit adds the Raspberry Pi 5, official power, and official case. It does not include the 978 whip or the hard case.
