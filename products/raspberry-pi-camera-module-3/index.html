FAQ

Is this a thermal camera?

No. Visible CSI only. Heat contrast is the 32×24 I2C module, the 160×120 USB module, or the handheld thermals.

Is this the Wide 120° module?

No. This is the standard 75° Camera Module 3. The Wide is a separate $89 page.

Does it plug into a Raspberry Pi 5?

The ribbon is 15-pin. A Pi 5 camera port is 22-pin. You may need a 22-pin to 15-pin adapter (about $3). We do not sell that adapter as its own SKU.

Is a battery included?

No. The module takes power from the Pi CSI port. No cell in the box.

Is this a drone camera you fly?

No. We do not fly this. It is a module for a Pi you already have, including the listener kits on this site.

Is this medical?

No. Visible site picture only. Not a medical device.

Is the HQ camera on this page?

No. We do not list the HQ body here — that body ships with no lens.
