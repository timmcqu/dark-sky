FAQ

Is this a handheld thermal camera?

No. It is a 32×24 module. The handheld 256-class camera is $549. The handheld 320-class camera is $2,199.

Is a cable or host included?

No. Module only. No cable, no host, no cell.

Can I use it as a medical thermometer?

No. Heat contrast only. Not a medical device.

What host do I need?

A 3.3 V I2C host with enough RAM to hold the frame. A classic Uno-class board is not enough.
