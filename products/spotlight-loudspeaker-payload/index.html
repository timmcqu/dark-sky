FAQ

No. This is a payload. It hangs on a contractor aircraft the buyer already has. Dark Sky Systems LLC does not sell the airframe.

Yes. You supply the airframe. We ship the spotlight and loudspeaker payload. If you do not already have a compatible aircraft, do not buy this SKU as a substitute for one.

No. It lights a lane and talks to the ground. It does not jam, spoof, or send a C-UAS command.

No. It is a payload for an aircraft, not a handheld light and not a handheld megaphone.
