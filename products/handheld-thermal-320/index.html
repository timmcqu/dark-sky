FAQ

Is this 640?

Native sensor is 320×240.

Is this a firefighting camera?

It is a handheld inspection camera (IP67) with a 300-lumen work light. The maker does not rate it as a firefighting TIC.

Is this a medical device?

No. Heat contrast only.

Does the extra FLIR battery fit?

No. The $109 extra battery is for FLIR E5 Pro / E6 Pro / E8 Pro only. It will not fit a Seek Reveal 300 or a TOPDON TC004.

How is this different from the $549 256-class?

This is 320×240, IP67, and a pocket brick with a work light. The $549 TOPDON TC004 is 256×192, IP54, pistol-grip, wider field of view.

Is the 32×24 module the same camera?

No. mlx32 is a 32×24 I2C module — no screen, no cell, no handle. Different SKU.
