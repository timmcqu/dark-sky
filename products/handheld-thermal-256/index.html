FAQ

Is this a medical thermometer?

No. Heat contrast for equipment and site picture. It is not a medical device.

Is this a firefighting helmet camera?

It is a handheld inspection camera (IP54) for heat contrast on site. The maker does not rate it as a firefighting TIC.

Is the sensor 512 or 640?

Native sensor is 256×192. TISR 512×384 is processing, not a 512-pixel sensor.

Does the extra FLIR battery fit?

No. The $109 extra battery is for FLIR E5 Pro / E6 Pro / E8 Pro only. It will not fit a TOPDON TC004 or a Seek Reveal 300. Both of those have a built-in cell.

How is this different from the 32×24 module?

The $149 row is a 32×24 I2C module — no screen, no cell, no handle. You wire it to a host. This SKU is a handheld camera you pick up.

Is a spare pack included?

No. Built-in cell only. We do not sell a TC004 spare pack.
