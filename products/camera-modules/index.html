FAQ

Which one is thermal?

The $149 32×24 I2C module, the $649 160×120 USB module, and the handhelds at $549 / $2,199. The $59 / $89 CSI cameras and the $89 USB board are visible light only.

Do I need a Pi 5 adapter?

Only for the two CSI Camera Module 3 pages. The ribbon is 15-pin. A Pi 5 camera port is 22-pin. About $3. We do not sell that adapter as its own SKU. The USB board does not need it.

Where is the HQ camera?

Not on this site. The HQ body ships with no lens. We did not list it.

Is any of this medical?

No. Visual site picture or heat contrast. Not a thermometer you use on a patient.
