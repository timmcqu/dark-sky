FAQ

Are any of these pre-flashed?

No. Not the $39 board, not the $79 color scanner, not the $89 mesh node, not the $129 display kit, not the $179 site module. Hardware as the shop ships it. Software is on you, or a later digital image pack we do not mint on this page.

Which rung should I buy?

Good $39 is a bare listen board. Better color $79 is one piece with a screen. Mesh $89 adds a flanged box and a backhaul radio that is not Remote ID. Display kit $129 is the bare board plus a separate OLED and a small box. Best sit $179 is the large IP66 box, PoE-to-USB-C power, and an external 2.4 GHz whip.

Is there an ESP32 spectrum board?

No. We do not sell an ESP32 spectrum-awareness board. The spectrum scanner is the TinySA Ultra.

Do you include a battery?

No. USB-C only. We refuse LiPo add-ons on every ESP32 rung.

Where are the Raspberry Pi kits?

The $399 Pi receiver and the $279 Pi starter live on their own pages. This ladder is ESP32 boards only.
