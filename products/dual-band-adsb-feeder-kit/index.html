FAQ

Is this the $199 ADS-B + Remote ID kit?

No. That kit is a Remote ID board, a listen radio, 1090 and 978, and a hard case — no computer. This kit is a Raspberry Pi 4 feeder with a listen radio and both band whips. No Remote ID board. No case.

Can I listen 1090 and 978 at the same time?

Not with the one radio in this kit. The two whips are SMA male; the radio is SMA female. Swap the antenna, or use a switch you already have. We are not shipping two radios.

Is there a Remote ID board?

No. If you need Remote ID on a Pi, see the $279 starter, the $399 Pi 5 receiver, or the $549 professional sit.

Is there a filter or an outdoor 1090?

No. No dual-channel filter (that is the $549 professional sit). No N-female outdoor 1090 (that is the $649 outdoor listener).

Is there a flashed image or a battery?

No. Hardware only. Official 15 W USB-C. You load your own feeder software. Not Fusion Sensor. Not V4.

Hardware Only or Ready-to-Run?
