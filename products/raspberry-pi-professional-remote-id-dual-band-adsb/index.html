FAQ

Why is this $549?

The dual-channel filter is about $95 of the kit. The $399 Pi 5 receiver stops at 1090 and has no filter. This kit adds the 978 whip and that filter. We do not hide the cost.

Is this a new wrapper on the $399 kit?

No. The $399 kit is Pi 5 + 27 W + official case + Remote ID board + radio + 1090 whip. This kit adds the 978 whip and the dual-channel filter.

Is the case weatherproof?

No. Official indoor clip-together ABS with a fan. For a weather box and a mast-mount 1090, see the outdoor permanent receiver.

Do I get two radios?

No. One radio, two band whips, and the filter on that radio. Remote ID is a separate board. You choose how to kit 1090 and 978.

Is there a battery or a flashed image?

No. Official 27 W USB-C. No Li-ion. Hardware only. Not Fusion Sensor. Not V4.

Hardware Only or Ready-to-Run?
