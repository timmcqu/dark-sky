FAQ

Is the duck SMA?

No. The indoor duck is RP-SMA male. A radio with SMA needs an adapter you already have or will add later. We do not ship an adapter in this SKU.

Is the outdoor omni SMA?

No. It is N-Female. Same as the $99 directional panel. You may need an N-to-SMA jumper.

Do these transmit or jam?

No. Passive receive only.

Which one should I buy?

Duck ($59) for a radio on a bench or a bag. Outdoor omni ($79) for a pole. Directional panel ($99) when you want gain in one direction.
