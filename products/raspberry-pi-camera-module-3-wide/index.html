FAQ

Is this a thermal camera?

No. Visible CSI only. Heat contrast is the 32×24 I2C module, the 160×120 USB module, or the handheld thermals.

How is this different from the $59 Camera Module 3?

Same 12 MP sensor. This SKU is the 120° Wide. The $59 page is the 75° standard.

Does it plug into a Raspberry Pi 5?

The ribbon is 15-pin. A Pi 5 camera port is 22-pin. You may need a 22-pin to 15-pin adapter (about $3). We do not sell that adapter as its own SKU.

Will it plug into a laptop USB?

No. CSI only. The USB low-light board is a different page.

Is a battery included?

No. Power comes from the Pi CSI port. No cell in the box.

Is this a drone camera you fly?

No. We do not fly this. It is a module for a Pi you already have.

Is this medical?

No. Visible site picture only.
