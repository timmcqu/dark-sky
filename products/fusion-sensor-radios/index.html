What you see, and what you do not

Green is on your list. Red is transmitting and not on your list. A quiet map is not a clearance. Receive-only.

What the Console hears

Remote IDDrones that are broadcasting the required US Remote ID message. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, depending on the radio you plug in.
ADS-B 1090Aircraft transponder broadcasts on 1090 MHz. Position, altitude, and callsign when the aircraft sends them.
978 MHz (UAT)US 978 MHz aircraft and weather broadcasts, when a 978 radio or dual-band filter is connected.
Radio energy (optional)The USB receive radio can show that energy is on its band. That is not an aircraft ID and not a range.
Operator locationShown when the Remote ID message includes the operator’s location. We do not find the operator by direction-finding.

Lists and packs

Friendly listYour spreadsheet of serials and callsigns you own. Green is on that list. We do not invent friendlies.
Remote ID fieldsID, location, operator, and self-ID exactly as sent. No invented tracks.
ADS-B fieldsAddress, callsign, position, and altitude when the aircraft sends them.
Fingerprint pack (optional)A make/family hint against a broadcast that is already on the map. Not a certified detector. Does not find dark aircraft.
Vendor pack (optional)A make table (DJI, Autel, FPV, ENG) applied only after a Remote ID or energy look already exists.
Keep-out boxesBoxes you name. Alert when a classified track is inside. We do not guess your keep-out.
Not includedLive FAA airspace query, FAA registry scrape, and third-party research radio libraries. Not in this license.

Rules we follow

US Remote ID (14 CFR Part 89)Console receives broadcasts. It does not enforce Remote ID and is not a substitute for it.
ASTM F3411The US Remote ID message set, mapped as sent.
OpenDroneIDThe public Remote ID format over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, radio dependent.
ADS-B 1090 / 978Standard aircraft broadcasts. 1090 is Mode S; 978 is US UAT.
