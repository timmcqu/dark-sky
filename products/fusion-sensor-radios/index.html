Airspace analysis

Datasets, packs, and fields Console uses to classify cooperative air. Receive-only. Fingerprints corroborate a look — they do not invent a track.

Receive layers

Remote ID (cooperative)ASTM F3411 / OpenDroneID messages that arrive. Wi-Fi NaN, BLE 4 legacy advertising, BLE 5 Coded PHY when the radio supports it.
ADS-B 1090 ESMode S extended squitter, RTCA DO-260B. ICAO 24-bit address, position, altitude, callsign when sent.
UAT 978RTCA DO-282B. US FIS-B / ADS-B UAT when a 978 radio or dual-band filter is connected.
RF energy (optional)Listen-radio bands only. CFAR / energy presence. Corroboration of a look — not an aircraft ID, not a range.
Operator locationOpenDroneID System / Location operator lat-lon when the RID message includes it. Not DF.

Datasets and packs

Friendly-list packClient-owned CSV: uas_id, callsign, operator, note. Match on serial / CAA / session ID. We do not invent friendlies.
RID field dictionaryOpenDroneID Basic ID, Location, System, Self-ID, Operator ID mapped 1:1. No synthesized tracks.
ADS-B decode pack1090 ES / UAT 978 field set: ICAO, callsign, lat/lon, baro/GNSS alt, NAC, emitter category when present.
device_fingerprint packOptional 50-class RF prior (soft vote). Corroborates a cooperative look or energy family. Not certified detect. Holdout is library regression, not an operational Pd.
drone_rf_id packOptional family prior (soft). Make / family vocabulary against a broadcast or energy look. Never a dark-aircraft detector.
Vendor / prior packOptional make-family table (DJI, Autel, FPV, ENG) applied only after a RID or energy look exists. Prior, not a track generator.
Keep-out / geofence packClient-named boxes. Alert when a classified track is inside. We do not guess keep-out.
Not loadedFAA LAANC live query, FAA registry scrape, RFUAV bulk IQ (~583 GB), and any third-party licensed research corpus. Not resold. Not in this license.

Classification

FRIENDLY (green)Usable RID ID on the friendly-list pack.
UNKNOWN RID (red)Usable RID ID, not on the list. Alert.
NO USABLE ID (amber)RID message arrived without a usable ID. Alert.
ADS-B1090 / 978 cooperative aircraft. Not scored as RID unknown.
RF energyEnergy look without RID. Displayed as corroboration / unknown energy — never ENEMY, never a house.
CLEARValid. No live cooperative tracks. Quiet is not a clearance of dark / RID-off aircraft.
Age-outTrack drops when last_message > 20 s (TRACK_STALE_S) or the feed dies.
