FAQ

Is this a handheld thermal camera?

No. It is a board plus a core. No screen, no handle, no cell. The $549 and $2,199 pages are the handhelds.

Is this the $149 32×24 module?

No. That row is 32×24 over I2C (768 points). This is 160×120 over USB (19,200 points).

Is a battery included?

No. USB power only. We do not ship a cell with this module.

Is a USB cable in the box?

No. The board’s locking connector is a small JST-style jack. Through-hole USB pads are on the board if you solder. We did not add a cable to this order.

Is this a medical thermometer?

No. Heat contrast only. Do not use it on a patient as a medical device.

Is this a firefighting camera?

No. It is a module. Not a certified TIC and not a helmet cam.

Why isn’t this $549?

$549 is the 256-class handheld. This module is $649 so the two cards do not collide.
