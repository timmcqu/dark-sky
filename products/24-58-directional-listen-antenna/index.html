FAQ

Does this transmit or jam?

No. Passive receive only. It is an antenna. It does not make RF.

Is the connector SMA?

No. N-Female. A listen radio with SMA needs an N-to-SMA jumper. We do not ship that jumper in this SKU.

How is this different from the $59 / $79 antennas?

Those are omnis — indoor RP-SMA duck at $59, outdoor N-Female stick at $79. This $99 panel is directional. Point it.

Is a jumper included?

No. Panel and pole hardware only.

Indoor or outdoor?

Weatherproof radome. Outdoor or indoor. Mast clamp is for a 30–50 mm pole.

Will it hear Remote ID by itself?

No. It is an antenna. Pair it with a listen radio or the $199 kit. The Dronetag RIDER already has its own antenna.
