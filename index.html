About

Dark Sky Systems LLC is a Texas company. Clients hire us for overhead imaging and a written risk assessment over a named venue and window.

We size each job the way emergency services size rope rescue, confined space, and hazmat. People come first, then the scene, then whether the aircraft leaves the ground. Detection is receive-only. Private and VIP work is discreet; you do not have to name the person.

Who flies

When we hire, we look first to people with a law-enforcement, fire, EMS, or military background. People who are still serving may fly only if their department allows outside work.

An FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate is required. A badge or a uniform does not replace it. When you fly for Dark Sky, you are not flying as that agency.

If that is your background, send a request below and mark Pilot.
