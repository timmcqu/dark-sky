Fusion Sensor

The product. A listen console on a laptop. Manned traffic, drone Remote ID, radio energy, and airports on one map. Built on thousands of hours of real sits and test ranges.

What you see

Airliners and other ADS-B traffic. Drones that broadcast Remote ID. Airports and runways. Radio contacts in red.

What the radio does

It scans 2.4 GHz and 5.8 GHz. It matches analog FPV and hopping digital links. It logs time, band, and energy.

Why it names things

Twenty training packs ship with the license. Fingerprints, visual track, and field evolutions.
