About

Dark Sky Systems LLC is a Texas company. Overhead imaging, an air map, and a separate site survey when that is the job.

We can bring CyberRaptor so you see the air as well as the ground. VIP work is discreet.

Who flies

When we hire, we look first to people with a law-enforcement, fire, EMS, or military background. Serving people may fly if their department allows outside work.

An FAA Part 107 certificate is required. A badge does not replace it. When you fly for Dark Sky, you are not flying as that agency. If that is your background, mark Pilot on the form below.
