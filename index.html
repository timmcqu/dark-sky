About

Dark Sky Systems LLC is a Texas company. Clients hire us for overhead imaging, a Full Site Picture of the air, and a written risk assessment over a named venue and window.

Operators can bring CyberRaptor, our RF listen platform, so the client sees what is broadcasting and what is emitting in the tuned bands, fused with the picture of the ground. We size each job the way emergency services size rope rescue, confined space, and hazmat. VIP work is discreet; you do not have to name the person.

Who flies

When we hire, we look first to people with a law-enforcement, fire, EMS, or military background. People who are still serving may fly only if their department allows outside work.

An FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate is required. A badge or a uniform does not replace it. When you fly for Dark Sky, you are not flying as that agency.

If that is your background, send a request below and mark Pilot.
