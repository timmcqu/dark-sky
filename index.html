Risk

We look at a scene the way firefighters do. People first. Then weather, access for ambulances, batteries, and other aircraft.

You get that in writing before we launch. If the day changes, we write the change. This is not a security company. We do not promise the event will be safe.

  • Site walk before launch
  • Written risks you initial
  • Stop-flying rules on paper
  • Battery fire and medical cards

About

Dark Sky Systems is a Texas LLC. We fly drones and write risk reports.

Rope rescue, confined space, hazmat. We size the job that way. We only listen. VIP jobs stay off the internet.

Who flies

We hire pilots from law enforcement, fire, EMS, and the military first. That includes people still serving, if their department or command allows outside work.

You still need an FAA Part 107 certificate to fly for us. A badge or a uniform does not replace it. When you fly for Dark Sky, you are not flying as that agency.

If that is your background, send a request below and check Pilot.
