Site survey

We walk the property, fly it, and write the observation file. Overhead stills of people, excavations, confined space, exit routes, and the path first-in companies use.

Operations keep this with the emergency action plan they already owe. Dated maps of the hole, the lip, ventilation, and who would have to come out of it.

  • Emergency action plan. Escape routes, workplace maps, accounting for people — 29 CFR 1910.38 and 1926.35.
  • Excavation / trench. Protective system, ladder, lip — 1926.651–.652.
  • Confined space. Limited entry, ventilation, rescue sketch — 1926 Subpart AA and 1910.146.
  • First-in. Aerial of the hole, grade staging, patient-out. Same picture a trench or confined-space rescue uses.

Not an OSHA inspection. Not a PE stamp. We do not write the employer’s plan.
