FAQ

What is in the Pocket box?
The Pocket Remote ID receiver with a 1.14 inch color screen, three buttons, and USB-C. No LiPo. No extra cable required if you have USB-C.
How fast does it ship?
Hardware Only after Stripe from US shops. Ready-to-Run after our bench check, usually one extra business day.
Where do you ship?
US, CA, MX, GB, IE, DE, FR, NL, AU, NZ. Stripe checkout.
Hardware Only vs Ready-to-Run?
Same radios. Ready-to-Run is our image and a bench check. Labor, not a radio markup. Pocket Ready-to-Run is $129. Hardware Only is $79.
Returns?
Unused Hardware Only can be refused if the shop allows it. Ready-to-Run is flashed for you; write if the unit is dead on arrival.
Do you jam or take over drones?
No. We do not jam, spoof, intercept, or take over. You own the radios.
Does a receiver find every drone?
No. Cooperative Remote ID and ADS-B only. Dark or RID-off aircraft are not in the picture.
If people are in danger?
Call 911 first. Dark Sky Systems LLC is not dispatch and not a licensed security company.
What is Field Pad?
A $5,900 device we build. ESP32-S3 for cooperative Remote ID. PortaPack Mayhem H4M for 1 MHz–6 GHz — docked it is the /console/ strip, undocked it is the handheld. Cradle in the case. Same glass as /console/. No BYO laptop. Dark / RID-off aircraft are not on the map unless they broadcast.
