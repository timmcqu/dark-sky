How to fill an observation file

This pack is the paper first-in companies and site operations keep with the emergency action plan. Walk or fly the named property. Write what you see. Date the stills. Do not invent what is not in the photograph.

Order of work

1. Name the property, the date, and who keeps the file.

2. Photograph people, ways out, holes, confined space, medical access, and the path apparatus uses.

3. Fill one row per finding. Still numbers go in the last column.

4. Keep the file with the site emergency action plan. Update it when the ground changes.

What belongs in the file

People. Who is on the roof, in the hole, or not at the assembly point.

Egress. Gates, ladders, pinch points, crowd queues.

Excavation. Ladder, box, lip, spoil.

Confined space. Limited entry, vent, retrieval — describe, do not classify.

Medical. Ambulance in, patient out, LZ if marked.

Limits

This is an observation template. It is not an inspection, a PE stamp, or the employer’s emergency action plan. Radio / Dark Sky Console is a separate product.
