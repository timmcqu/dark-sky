RF monitoring

Receive-only radio picture of a named place and time. Energy in the bands we are watching is logged. Quiet air is a valid hour.

This is the core of the platforms. A drone that is not broadcasting Remote ID can still radiate on a control or video link. RF monitoring is how that energy shows up. It is presence and band, not a tail number and not a legal intercept order.

Bands

We watch the bands on that job. Common air links sit in 2.4 GHz and 5.8 GHz. Other bands are added when the kit on site actually covers them.

What you see

Time, band, and energy. Repeat hits stay on the log. A dead feed drops off. We do not invent tracks to fill a quiet hour.
