TFR

Many U.S. stadium windows carry a published TFR. Read the NOTAM. We do not sell a bubble. We do not neutralize.

If people are in danger, call 911 first. Dark Sky Systems LLC is not a licensed security company.

What you hear

Pocket is a 1.14 inch color screen for cooperative Remote ID. Field Pad is the live /console/ glass: satellite map plus spectrum waterfall. Green is on your friendly list. Red is transmitting and not on the list. Rings are scale, not range. Energy is not an ID.

FAQ

Do you keep drones out of the stadium?
No. We do not jam, spoof, or take over. A receiver hears cooperative broadcasts. Quiet is not a clearance.
What is a stadium TFR?
A public FAA temporary flight restriction around many stadium events (often 3 NM, ±1 hour). It is not a product we sell.
What do I buy?
Pocket $129 for a color screen. Console $2,000 is the /console/ map on a host you own. Field Pad $4,950 adds the spectrum strip. Ready $6,900 ships that same build on a mini-PC.
Will the picture include dark aircraft?
No. Cooperative Remote ID and, when the kit is on, ADS-B. Quiet is not a clearance.
