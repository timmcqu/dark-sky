FAQ

Can one board cover a whole campus?

No. One Remote ID receiver is one kit. Large campuses add nodes or a Pi plus ESP32 site receivers.

Is this licensed security?

No. Dark Sky Systems LLC is not a licensed security company. We sell receive hardware and coverage.

Do you fly every week?

Campus and Facility Assurance is four flown visits a year. Monitoring is a map. They are different SKUs.
