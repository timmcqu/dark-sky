ADS-B feeder kit

The ADS-B feeder kit is a Raspberry Pi 4 kit for 1090 and 978 aircraft broadcasts. Radio and both band whips. No Remote ID board. $299 / $369.

What you hear

Crewed ADS-B. Not drones. For Remote ID plus 1090, buy the Raspberry Pi Remote ID receiver kit instead. Product: Dual-Band ADS-B Feeder Kit.

One radio, two whips

1090 and 978 are not simultaneous on one radio without a switch or a second radio. The kit includes both whips. Say that on the install card.
