Construction and jobsite drone detection

A jobsite needs a Remote ID receiver that can live in a trailer, a log the superintendent can print, and honesty about what the kit cannot see. Dark Sky sells that kit. We do not sell a jammer.

Most jobs buy the Radio starter or the ESP32 Remote ID scanner. A longer job with power: a Raspberry Pi ADS-B starter. Paper: Jobsite drone log ($19) and the incident file.

Progress flights are separate

Construction Progress is overhead imaging on a weekly cadence. It is not the receiver kit. Buy both if the job needs both.

FAQ

Will this stop a drone over the crane?

No. This is receive-only drone detection. It does not jam, spoof, or intercept.

Do I need ADS-B on a jobsite?

Only if you care about crewed traffic. Remote ID is the drone layer.

Can the superintendent run it?

Yes. Ready-to-Run if they should not flash a card. Hardware Only if they already do.
